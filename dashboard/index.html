How it works

Three checks, milliseconds each

You don't change your model or your prompts. Michael-V1 sits around the agent's tools.

1

Read safely

Emails, web pages and files are scanned the moment they arrive. Hidden instructions are quarantined and secrets are hidden before the AI reads them.

2

Act only on your orders

Before an email, payment or file share, the target is traced to its source. If it came from something the AI read, and not from you, it's blocked.

3

Tell the truth

If the AI says "I've sent it" or "payment done", Michael-V1 checks the real action log and warns you when it never happened.
