    Versus the field

    open-source agent guardrails, compared honestly
    01

    Asks "who chose this target?"

    Every address, account and file the agent acts on is traced to its source. Believable phishing that every AI detector scores as safe still gets stopped.

    02

    Catches lies about actions

    "I've sent it" is checked against the real action log by code. An AI never grades itself.

    03

    Milliseconds, not seconds

    Rules first, scan-on-arrival, AI checks in parallel. No AI call blocks a read; risky actions fail closed.

    04

    Speaks Hinglish & Hindi

    Jailbreaks in Indian languages are caught, normal Hindi requests are not blocked.

    05

    Sees through disguises

    Decodes base64, strips invisible characters and spots split-up or fake-authority commands before judging.

    06

    India-ready leak guard

    Aadhaar validated with UIDAI's Verhoeff checksum, PAN, card numbers with Luhn: fewer false alarms.
